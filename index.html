VEC Winter Reporting

Internal analytics for the Varsity Esports Championship team

What this is

VEC Winter Reporting is an internal reporting tool for the Varsity Esports Championship (VEC) team. With the account owner's authorization, it reads the team's own TikTok profile statistics and video performance metrics through TikTok's official Login Kit and Display API, and compiles them into a seasonal performance report. It only ever accesses the TikTok account that explicitly authorizes it.
