Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

1. Introduction

VEC Winter Reporting ("the tool") is an internal analytics tool operated by the Varsity Esports Championship (VEC) team. This policy explains what data the tool collects from TikTok, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

2. Data we collect

The tool collects data only from the TikTok account that explicitly authorizes it through TikTok's official Login Kit. No data is collected from any other account. The data collected is:

  • Profile information (Login Kit scope user.info.profile): display name, avatar, bio.
  • Account statistics (Login Kit scope user.info.stats): follower count, following count, total likes received, video count.
  • Own video metrics (Display API scope video.list): per-video title, cover image, publish time, view count, like count, comment count, and share count.

We do not collect passwords, private messages, or data from any account that has not authorized the tool.

3. How we use the data

Collected data is used solely to compile seasonal performance reports for the VEC team's internal use (for example, tracking the reach and engagement of the team's own TikTok content across a season). Data is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share collected data with third parties. Data is accessed only by members of the VEC team involved in performance reporting. The only external service involved is TikTok itself, whose APIs provide the data under the account owner's authorization and TikTok's own Privacy Policy.

5. Data storage and security

Collected metrics are stored in the team's private reporting spreadsheet and access credentials are kept in access-controlled storage. Reasonable technical measures are applied to protect stored data against unauthorized access.

6. Data retention

Performance metrics are retained for the duration of the reporting season plus up to 12 months for year-over-year comparison, after which aggregated summaries may be kept and raw per-video data deleted on request.

7. Your rights

  • Revoke access: you can revoke the tool's access at any time in your TikTok account settings (Settings > Security > Manage app permissions). Revocation stops all further collection immediately.
  • Deletion: contact us at the email below to request deletion of data collected from your account.
  • Access: contact us to request a copy of the data the tool holds about your account.

8. Children's privacy

The tool is operated for the VEC team's internal use and is not directed at children. If you believe data has been collected in error, contact us and it will be removed.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated effective date above.
