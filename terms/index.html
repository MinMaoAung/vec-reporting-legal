1. Description of the service

VEC Winter Reporting ("the tool") is an internal analytics tool operated by the Varsity Esports Championship (VEC) team. After the TikTok account owner grants authorization through TikTok's official Login Kit, the tool reads that account's own profile statistics and video performance metrics through TikTok's Display API and compiles them into a seasonal performance report for internal use.

2. Eligibility

You may connect a TikTok account to the tool only if you own or are authorized to manage that account. The tool never accesses any TikTok account other than the one that explicitly completes the authorization flow.

3. TikTok platform terms

Use of TikTok data through the tool is subject to TikTok's own Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Developer Terms. Authorization can be revoked at any time from within your TikTok account settings; revoking access stops all further data collection by the tool.

4. Acceptable use

  • Do not attempt to connect TikTok accounts you do not own or manage.
  • Do not attempt to circumvent TikTok's authorization flow or access controls.
  • Do not use data obtained through the tool for purposes other than the VEC team's internal performance reporting.

5. Data

The tool reads the following data from the authorized TikTok account only: public profile information (display name, avatar, bio), account statistics (follower count, following count, total likes, video count), and per-video metrics (views, likes, comments, shares, title, cover image, publish time). How this data is handled is described in our Privacy Policy.
